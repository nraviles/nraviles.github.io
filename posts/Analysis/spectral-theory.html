Bounded Operators

Let \(H\) be a Hilbert space over \(\C\). Recall our global macro test: \(x \in \R^n\) and \(\inner{u}{v}\).

Definition 1 (Spectrum) Let \(T \in \End(H)\). The spectrum of \(T\), denoted \(\Spec(T)\), is the set of all \(\lambda \in \C\) such that \(T - \lambda I\) is not invertible.1
